The Product and Quotient Rules

In this section you will find examples of calculating derivatives with the product and quotient rules, together with practice problems. Try to solve each problem yourself first, then reveal the solution one step at a time.

  • Rules
  • Examples
  • Practice

The rules used

The product rule

\[ \begin{aligned} \big(u(x)\cdot v(x)\big)' &= u'(x)\cdot v(x) \\ &\phantom{=}\; + u(x)\cdot v'(x) \end{aligned} \]

The quotient rule

\[ \left(\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}\right)' = \frac{u'(x)v(x) - u(x)v'(x)}{v^2(x)} \]

In a product, either factor may be labelled \(u(x)\); in a quotient, \(u(x)\) is always the numerator and \(v(x)\) the denominator. The rules are explained, with detailed examples, in the product rule and quotient rule cards on the Topic 6 page.

Examples

Example 1

A product of a power and a logarithm

Problem Find \(f'(x)\) if \(f(x) = x^2\ln x\).

1The rules

Both factors depend on \(x\), so apply the product rule. In the rule, use \(u(x) = x^2\) and \(v(x) = \ln x\) as the two factors. First differentiate the power and multiply by the unchanged logarithm. Then add the unchanged power multiplied by the derivative of the logarithm.

2Differentiation

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= (x^2)'\cdot\ln x + x^2\cdot(\ln x)' \\ &= 2x\ln x + x^2\cdot\frac{1}{x} \end{aligned} \]

Write these two terms before simplifying; differentiating both factors in a single product would give an incorrect result.

3Simplifying

In the second term, one power of \(x\) cancels with the denominator. Both remaining terms share this factor, so factor it out to obtain the final expression:

\[ f'(x) = 2x\ln x + x = x(2\ln x + 1) \]

The expanded and factored answers are equivalent.

Answer \[ f'(x) = x(2\ln x + 1) \]
Example 2

A quotient of two polynomials

Problem Find \(f'(x)\) if \(f(x) = \dfrac{2x + 1}{x^2 + 3}\).

1The rules

The denominator depends on \(x\), so apply the quotient rule. Use \(u(x) = 2x + 1\) for the numerator and \(v(x) = x^2 + 3\) for the denominator in the rule. Their derivatives are \(u'(x) = 2\) and \(v'(x) = 2x\). Form the derivative of the numerator times the unchanged denominator, then subtract the unchanged numerator times the derivative of the denominator. Divide this entire difference by the square of the original denominator.

2Differentiation

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \frac{(2x + 1)'(x^2 + 3) - (2x + 1)(x^2 + 3)'}{(x^2 + 3)^2} \\ &= \frac{2(x^2 + 3) - (2x + 1)\cdot 2x}{(x^2 + 3)^2} \end{aligned} \]

3Simplifying

Expand both products in the numerator. The subtraction applies to the entire second product, so each of its terms changes sign. Combine like terms, then factor out \(-2\) if desired:

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \frac{2x^2 + 6 - 4x^2 - 2x}{(x^2 + 3)^2} \\ &= \frac{-2x^2 - 2x + 6}{(x^2 + 3)^2} \\ &= \frac{-2(x^2 + x - 3)}{(x^2 + 3)^2} \end{aligned} \]

Keep the denominator squared; no common factor cancels in this example.

Answer \[ f'(x) = \frac{-2(x^2 + x - 3)}{(x^2 + 3)^2} \]
Example 3

A quotient of a root and a binomial

Problem Find \(f'(x)\) if \(f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{x + 1}\).

1The rules

Apply the quotient rule, using \(u(x) = \sqrt{x}\) for the numerator and \(v(x) = x + 1\) for the denominator. Differentiate the square root using the table; the derivative of the denominator is one. Substitute these derivatives into the rule before simplifying.

2Differentiation

\[ f'(x) = \frac{\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\cdot(x + 1) - \sqrt{x}\cdot 1}{(x + 1)^2} \]

3Simplifying

To clear the fraction within the numerator, multiply the overall numerator and denominator by \(2\sqrt{x}\). This preserves the value of the fraction, and \(\sqrt{x}\cdot\sqrt{x} = x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \frac{(x + 1) - 2\sqrt{x}\cdot\sqrt{x}}{2\sqrt{x}(x + 1)^2} \\ &= \frac{x + 1 - 2x}{2\sqrt{x}(x + 1)^2} \\ &= \frac{1 - x}{2\sqrt{x}(x + 1)^2} \end{aligned} \]

The derivative is valid for \(x > 0\).

Answer \[ f'(x) = \frac{1 - x}{2\sqrt{x}(x + 1)^2} \]

Practice problems

Write the solutions out as in the examples: identify the structure of the function, choose the rule, differentiate and simplify the result. Then check your answer.

Find \(f'(x)\) if \(f(x) = x^3e^x\).

\(f'(x) = 3x^2e^x + x^3e^x\) \(= x^2e^x(3 + x)\)

Find \(f'(x)\) if \(f(x) = (x^2 - 1)\ln x\).

\(f'(x) = 2x\ln x + \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x}\)

Find \(f'(x)\) if \(f(x) = \dfrac{3x - 2}{x^2 + 1}\).

\(f'(x) = \dfrac{-3x^2 + 4x + 3}{(x^2 + 1)^2}\)
